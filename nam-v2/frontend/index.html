About Marswalk

A field atlas for Mars.

Explore NASA/JPL imagery, compare planetary datasets and sketch proposed traverses. Scientific layers retain their source names and coverage limits.

A drawn route is not a safety assessment. Surface hazards require separate, validated elevation and environmental datasets.
